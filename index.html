How Modern Tokenizers Work

Language models do not process raw text directly. Instead, tokenizers like Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE), WordPiece, and SentencePiece decompose text into integer token IDs. Common words remain single tokens, while rare or compound words are cleanly split into subwords.

Understanding Semantic Vector Spaces

Real embedding models (such as BERT or text-embedding-3) map sentences into 768 to 3072 dimensions. Dimensionality reduction techniques like PCA, t-SNE, and UMAP project those high-dimensional spaces down to 3D so humans can explore semantic neighborhoods.
